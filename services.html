Services

This page is the repair workshop. Shakur Grinding Service inspects and reconditions the precision tooling below. Easy Clamp, the patented holder, is made separately.

Microboring Head

Reconditioning of microboring heads so fine bore adjustments stay accurate on the machine.

Hydrogrip

Hydraulic tool-holder reconditioning that brings clamping grip and runout back into working order.

Live Tool

Repair of driven live-tool units used on turning centres, including bearings, seals, and drive parts.

Angular Head

Reconditioning of angle heads so right-angle and multi-axis machining stays rigid and true.

Hydraulic Vice

Hydraulic vice repair covering jaws, screws, seals, and clamping force for reliable workholding.

Mechanical Vice

Mechanical vice reconditioning to restore jaw alignment, screw travel, and repeatable clamping.

Tapping Attachment

Repair of tapping heads and attachments so thread cutting stays controlled and consistent.

CNC Router Motor

Reconditioning of CNC router spindle motors, including bearings and running accuracy.

Auto Clamping Hydrogrip

Reconditioning of auto-clamping hydrogrips and special hydrogrip holders built for automatic tool change.

Hydraulic Mandrel

Hydraulic mandrel repair so internal expanding grip holds bores without slip or distortion.

Locking Hub Nut

Reconditioning of hydraulic locking hub nuts used to lock spindles, cutters, and precision assemblies.

Hydraulic Chuck

Hydraulic chuck reconditioning for power chucks that must clamp, repeat, and release cleanly.

Hydraulic Lifter

Repair of hydraulic lifters and lifting assemblies used around machine tools and fixtures.

ADS Holder

ADS holder reconditioning to restore taper contact, clamping, and tool seating.

Shrink Fit Holder

Shrink-fit holder repair so heat-shrink clamping returns to a true, rigid grip.

Steady Rest (CNC)

CNC steady-rest reconditioning, including arms, rollers, and clamping action on long workpieces.

Work Rest

Hydraulic work-support and work-rest repair for fixtures that hold parts during machining.

Pneumatic Cylinder

Pneumatic cylinder reconditioning: seals, rods, and smooth stroke for air-powered clamping.

Hydraulic Cylinder

Hydraulic cylinder repair for machine-tool and fixture cylinders that leak, drift, or lose force.

Universal Boring & Facing Head

Reconditioning of universal boring and facing heads used for precision bore and face work.

Antivibration Tool Holder

Silent-tool and antivibration holder reconditioning for stable cutting on long reaches.

Vertex Punch Former

Repair of Vertex punch formers used in toolrooms for punch and electrode grinding setups.

Through Coolant Holder

Through-coolant holder reconditioning so internal coolant delivery seals and flows as it should.

Spindle Speeder

Spindle speeder repair to restore high-speed gearing, bearings, and smooth rotation.

MBU

Inspection and reconditioning of MBU precision units used in boring and production setups.

Hydraulic Clamp

Hydraulic clamp repair for swing clamps and fixture clamps that must hold without leak or play.

Power Milling Chuck

Power milling chuck reconditioning to recover gripping force and running truth.

Keyless Chuck

Keyless drill-chuck repair so the sleeve, jaws, and grip work without a chuck key.

Sidelock

Side-lock adapter reconditioning, including taper, lock screws, and bore condition.

Revolving Centre

Live-centre and revolving-centre repair so the point runs true under turning loads.
